# Changelog

## 0.8.2 Beta – faster screenshots, one-click updates

- Screenshots trigger right away (screen capture is prepared in advance, all displays are captured at once)
- Alpi no longer comes to the front when you take a screenshot; the app you're working in keeps its focus
- Smoother area selection on large Retina displays
- New: "Update Now" – Alpi downloads, verifies (Apple signature + notarization) and installs new versions itself, then restarts

## 0.8.1 Beta – faster, nicer editor

- Fixed: the editor stuttered or hung with large screenshots – drawing, dragging and zooming are smooth now
- Fixed: short freezes after a capture (copying, saving, clipboard history now work in the background)
- Image adjustments are calculated in the background while you drag
- Redesigned editor: dark workspace, tool palette with colour indicator, card-style sidebar that can be hidden, cleaner bottom bar
- Images copied from Alpi stay in the clipboard after quitting

## 0.8 Beta – Tidy up Downloads

- Sort your Downloads (or any folder) into Images, Videos, Audio, Documents, Archives, Installers, Code and Other
- Shows an overview first, never deletes anything, leaves your own subfolders and running downloads alone
- Undo the last sort with one click
- Ask Alpi: “Sort my Downloads”, “sort a folder”, “undo the sort”; also in the menu bar menu, Alpi’s right-click menu and the Shortcuts app
- Optional: sort new downloads automatically after a wait (right away, 1 hour, 1 day), leaving unopened files alone
- Folder sorting can be switched off completely
- Customizable keyboard shortcuts: give every action your own shortcut in Settings › Keyboard Shortcuts

## 0.7 Beta – first public beta

The first public version of Alpi. Free while it's in beta.

- Capture: area, window, full screen, scrolling capture (with auto-scroll), text from an area, self-timer
- Screen recording to MP4 or GIF with clicks, microphone, system audio and camera bubble
- Editor: annotations, redaction (auto-detects personal data and faces), frames, adjustments, history
- Orbit: screenshot cards, smart actions (text, QR, dates, tables), shelf, pinning
- Clipboard history, colour picker, QR codes, drawing on screen
- File conversion by drag & drop, including Office documents
- Ask Alpi, with Apple Intelligence on macOS 26/27 (image understanding on macOS 27)
- Folder rules and Shortcuts actions
- Privacy & security settings: history retention, clipboard clearing, link checks, shortcut confirmation
- In-app feedback and update notice

*Languages: English and German.*
